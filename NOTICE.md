# Notice and third-party data policy

JLA is an independent non-partisan research infrastructure. It is not an official Government of Jharkhand or Government of India product.

The JLA software, schemas, documentation and original derived products are released under CC BY 4.0. Source datasets are **not automatically relicensed** by inclusion in the source registry. A source may be referenced, transformed or redistributed only as permitted by its own terms.

The Core Geography baseline cites Census of India / Office of the Registrar General & Census Commissioner sources. Users must verify current administrative boundaries and source licences before redistributing third-party raw data.

JLA must not publish directly identifiable personal records in the public release. Missing data means unavailable/not validated, never zero.
